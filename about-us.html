❤️ Project Phoenix — Founding Principle

People Before Profit

Project Phoenix exists to help people, not profit from their struggles.

Our mission is to provide free, accessible resources to those seeking help, hope, and a better tomorrow.

We believe that someone reaching out for assistance should never have to navigate advertisements, commercial pressure, or financial barriers to find the support they need.

Our resources will remain free to access and free from advertising.

Let's leave the world better than we found it.
