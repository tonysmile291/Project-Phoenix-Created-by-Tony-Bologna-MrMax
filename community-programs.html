Start Making a Difference

Helping your community doesn't always require money or a large organization.

Sometimes it begins with checking on a neighbor, sharing useful information, volunteering an hour, organizing a neighborhood cleanup, or helping someone find the support they need.

Every act of kindness matters. Small actions can create lasting change.

Stay Safe While Helping

Before donating money, sharing personal information, or joining an unfamiliar organization, take time to verify that it is legitimate.

Be cautious of pressure to donate immediately, unexpected requests for payment, and promises that sound too good to be true.

Our Promise
